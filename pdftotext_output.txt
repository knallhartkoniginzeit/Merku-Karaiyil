Logline :

Set in 2014 Trichy, Ram aka Perry (13), Hari aka Keeri (13), and David aka Dhadi
(13) are three inseparable boys escaping broken homes and constant emotional
pressure, united by one small dream to see the sea once in their lives. When they
run away toward Varkala, fate brings them together with Chandru, a quiet,
emotionally distant man who unexpectedly becomes their guide and protector. As
the journey unfolds, Chandru witnesses their innocence, friendship, and unfiltered
joy, while silently battling a deep guilt he cannot share. After helping the boys
fulfill their dream, reunite with their families, and heal their wounds, Chandru
walks away alone, leaving behind a journey that seems to have changed all four of
their lives. But there is a reason Chandru found these three boys, a reason he
knows things about them he shouldn't and a secret from his past that connects
him to this journey in a way no one could have imagined.
Synopsis :

2014, Trichy.
Ram aka Perry (13), Hari aka Keeri (13), and David aka Dhadi (13) are inseparable friends. Life at home is
difficult for all three. Perry lives with a strict mother and an often-absent father, Keeri grows up surrounded by
constant fights, and Dhadi lives with his alcoholic father. Their homes are filled with tension, and the only place
they feel safe and happy is with each other. Whenever Perry feels overwhelmed, he escapes to an old theatre to
watch movies alone.
In the same colony lives Varipuli (24), a timid man with ADHD and Tourette syndrome who is looked down upon by
everyone, including his uncle, who runs a wholesale-load business using a Tata Ace. Though Varipuli does most
of the hard work, he is never respected. The three boys are the only ones who treat him with kindness, and their
friendship means everything to him.
After the half-yearly holidays, a rich and arrogant classmate, Arjun (13), mocks the boys for never having seen a
beach. He boasts about his family trip to Varkala, knowing they have never been there. Hurt and embarrassed, the
boys promise themselves that one day they will visit a beach, take photos, and prove him wrong.

Around the same time, Perry hears a rumour that actor Sivakarthikeyan will attend their school annual day. The
news spreads throughout the school, but on the day, the students discover that it was false. Arjun humiliates the
boys again, and the next day Perry loses his temper and hits him. Their parents are called, a heated argument
breaks out between the families, and the boys are forbidden from meeting each other. Perry's mother even
decides to change his school and move houses after the exams.
Fearing separation and fed up with their homes, the boys decide to run away after their exams and go to Varkala.
When Varipuli discovers their plan, he secretly agrees to help, believing it will only be a short trip.
Synopsis :

Meanwhile, Chandru (26), an IT professional, struggles with guilt from his past. One night, after
receiving a voice note from Kavya (25), who secretly loves him, Chandru avoids her birthday party
invitation and drives away in the rain. At the same time, Varipuli hides the boys in the back of his Tata
Ace and drives toward Kerala. After a misunderstanding at the Madurai toll, the boys get stranded on the
highway.

Later that night, Chandru attends Kavya's birthday celebration. Seeing her surrounded by happy friends
triggers painful memories, and when Kavya confesses her feelings, he walks away, believing he doesn't
deserve happiness. Lost in the rain, the boys suddenly appear in front of Chandru's speeding car. A
passing comet lights up the dark road just in time, allowing Chandru to stop inches away from them. The
boys lie about their situation and convince him to take them to Varkala. Reluctantly, he agrees.

The journey begins with constant clashes between Chandru and Perry. Meanwhile, their parents report
them missing to the police. During the journey, the boys help a girl injured in a road accident and take
her to the hospital, where they meet Murugan (35+), a stranded man who asks them for a ride to
Trivandrum. They agree, unaware that he is involved in statue smuggling.

Back in Trichy, Varipuli speaks honestly to the boys' parents about the pressure their children have been
living under. Slowly, the parents begin to realize their own mistakes.
Synopsis :

In Trivandrum, Murugan's actions lead the boys into trouble, and they are taken to a police station. There, they meet
Varadhan (45), who recognizes them from their school and rescues them. He takes them to his adopted daughter's
reception.
At the reception, the boys learn about Varadhan's troubled past, which unknowingly mirrors Chandru's own guilt. Their
presence brings Varadhan closer to his family. That night, Chandru gets drunk and opens up emotionally.
The next morning, Chandru becomes furious when he discovers that the boys went to a nearby lake and slaps Perry.
Hurt, the four silently leave Varadhan's house. Meanwhile, the parents learn that Varipuli knew about the boys' plan and
decide to travel to Kerala. The police advise them to go to Kerala the next morning.

That night, boys find a room to stay the night. Perry clashes with Chandru again and storms out, misunderstanding his
friends and leaving the room. The boys search desperately. Perry ends up in an old theatre, where a drunk man tries to
misbehave with him but others intervene. The boys finally find Perry at the theatre exit and reunite emotionally. Touched
by their bond, Chandru listens as the boys admit the truth they just want to go home. Chandru agrees to help them.

The next day, while their parents travel from Trichy, Chandru secretly takes the boys to Varkala instead. When they
realize it, the boys are overwhelmed. They run into the sea, laughing and playing freely. Chandru watches, smiling
through pain, haunted by memories of two friends who died years ago in an accident involving him. The boys hug him
and tell him it was not his fault. It becomes a healing moment for all of them. As Chandru goes to get towels, the
parents arrive at the beach. Expecting anger, the boys are met with hugs and apologies. Everyone forgives each other.
Chandru watches from a distance and quietly leaves, his heart finally at peace.
The boys return home with their parents. Chandru drives alone on the highway, tears in his eyes this time, happy ones.
A comet passes across the sky once again, marking a new beginning.
Synopsis :

A few days later, Chandru prepares for a date with Kavya. Before leaving his house, he looks
at a photo frame and smiles. The camera slowly reveals the photo it is Perry, Keeri, and Dhadi
in their school uniforms. As the camera moves around the room we see many photo frames on
the wall of Perry with his parents and pictures of Perry, Keeri, and Dhadi growing up together
from childhood to college. These visuals are intercut with Chandru's voice over, reflecting on
his journey and memories.
Chandru is no stranger.
He is Ram. His full name is Ramachandran.

Ragupathi (40) - Ragu is Perry's father, working as a project manager for a
car company in Mumbai. He is hardworking and loving, but his demanding job
keeps him away from his family. A passionate car enthusiast, he always
brings vintage car model collections for his son whenever he visits. Though
he struggles to build a strong bond with Perry, he genuinely wants to be a
better father and be more present in his life.

Shruthi (35) - Shruthi is Ragu's wife and Perry's mother. She is a bold,
hardworking woman who believes strictness and discipline are essential to
raising her son. Though she can be harsh with Perry, her actions come
from a deep concern for his future. She rarely shows affection in front of
him, but secretly worries and breaks down when she feels she has been
too harsh. Beneath her strict exterior is a deeply caring mother who simply
struggles to express her love.

Dinesh (38) - Dinesh is Keeri's father, a hardworking man who leaves home
early and returns late every day. Burdened by work pressure and family
responsibilities, he is easily irritated and often gets into arguments with his
wife. He loves her but never learned how to express it, and over time, that
love has faded beneath their constant conflicts. Detached from his children,
Dinesh longs for a peaceful life but never truly tries to create one.

Banumathi (32) - Banumathi is Dinesh's wife and Keeri's mother, a
working woman struggling to keep her middle-class family together.
Frustrated by her husband's behaviour and the pressures of everyday life,
she easily loses her temper and often takes it out on her children. She
rarely shows affection, carrying her own resentment quietly, especially
towards Perry's mother. She believes that one day, when life gets better,
she will finally become calm--but she fails to realise that kindness and
peace can exist even within her present circumstances.

Amul Doss (45) - Amul is Dhadi's father, a working-class man who runs a
small ready-made clothing shop in the bazaar. After losing his wife four years
ago, he has struggled to cope with the grief and turns to drinking every day.
He comes home late, avoids household responsibilities, and remains
emotionally distant from his son. Disliked and judged by the people in the
colony, Amul no longer cares about what they think of him. His unresolved
grief and drinking have deeply affected both his life and Dhadi's childhood.

Varadhan (50) - Varadhan is a former student of the three boys' school
and a successful textile businessman. A kind and gentle man, he runs a
trust for abandoned children with organisations in Trichy and Kerala.
Varadhan is a reflection of Chandru, carrying a similar guilt from his past.
Though he has never completely overcome it, he has learned to live with it
and continues to seek forgiveness for his mistakes. He believes the world
belongs to children, and that it is our responsibility to make it a better
place for them.

Director

Dilip

Dilip is an emerging writer, director, and actor from Trichy,
currently based in Chennai. He's driven by character-centred
storytelling and a fascination with the emotional complexities of
everyday life. With experience as an Assistant Director across
film projects and as a director of independent short films, he is
drawn to stories that find depth in ordinary people, relationships,
and seemingly simple moments. His cinematic sensibility
embraces natural landscapes, understated emotions, and the
delicate balance between humour and melancholy. "Merku
Karaiyil" marks his most ambitious step yet, bringing together the
innocence of childhood, the bond of friendship, the complexities
of family, the weight of guilt, and the quiet longing of waiting for
love, all unfolding against the beauty of the South Indian
landscape.
