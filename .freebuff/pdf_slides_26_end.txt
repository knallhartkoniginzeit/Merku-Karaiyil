The colour palette evolves with the story, reflecting the different phases of the narrative and
 the emotional journeys of the characters. Each palette is designed to organically shape the

   mood of the sequences and guide the audience through the emotional world of the film.

Color :

Warm, Saturated,
Red, Yellow, Green

The boyz portions should
carry a warm, sun soaked
summer palette that evokes
the nostalgia and innocence
of childhood.

The second half embraces a mixed
